Hello! My name is Alachie Yeager and I am a student at [Massachusetts Academy of Math and Sciences at WPI](https://www.massacademy.org"). 

I have historically pursued underwater robotics, working at my team at my previous school, [Medford High](https://mhs-mvths.mps02155.org). I first designed a vertically profiling float the traverses in the water column to collect samples at different depths. This float utilizes a piston-based boyancy engine for movement, and an pressure, pH, and turbidity sensor to measure water parameters.

I also historically have participated extensively in student government,serving as a student representative to Medford School commitee, as well as to regional and state-level assembelies. I spearheaded an effort to add a 7th period to the schoolday, which enabled vocation students who attend their shop half of the day to take their desired higher-level classes. We also reformed student demonstration policy, and overhauled the transportation system, allowing buses to reach more students for the same cost.

Lastly, I'm also an avid reader and cook. My favorite book is probably Ian Bank's *Surface Detail*, which is a sci-fi epic that explores the consequences of simulated afterlives. I also enjoy historical fiction like *All the Light We Cannot See*, among others. My favorite non-fiction book is *Germs, Guns, and Steel*. Among my favorite foods to cook are Enchiladas, Pad Kee Mao, Mushu, a fun caramalized pasta sauce I invented one time on acccident, and fried rice.
